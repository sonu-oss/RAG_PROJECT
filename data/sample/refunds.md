# Refunds

You can refund a captured payment in full or in part within 180 days. Refunds
usually reach the customer's bank in 5 to 10 business days. The processing fee
from the original charge is not returned.

Partial refunds can be issued several times until the total refunded equals
the captured amount. Trying to refund more than that returns `ERR-4220`.

## Disputes

If a customer contacts their bank instead of you, the bank opens a dispute
(also called a chargeback). You have 7 days to upload evidence such as receipts
or delivery confirmation. A dispute fee of $15 applies whether or not you win.
