Nimbus Pay FAQ

Which currencies are supported?
Nimbus Pay supports 38 currencies. Payouts are made in your account's settlement
currency; other currencies are converted at the daily mid-market rate plus 1%.

How long do payouts take?
Payouts reach your bank account on a rolling 2 business day schedule. New
accounts have a 7 day holding period for their first payout.

Can I test without moving real money?
Yes. Use a test key in the sandbox. The card number 4242 4242 4242 4242 always
succeeds and 4000 0000 0000 0002 is always declined.

Is my customers' card data stored on my servers?
No. Card details go straight from the browser to Nimbus Pay through the hosted
checkout form, so you stay in the lightest PCI compliance scope (SAQ A).
